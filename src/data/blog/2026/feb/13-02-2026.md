My first ever blog...so lemme keep it short. 

Heyya potato people, I'm Ganesh Macherla, more simply..people like to call me Machu.

I was a simple science student in the humble comfort of my hometown of Hyderabad, until one day I somehow got yeeted into the field of computers and thrown in a hostel in Chennai (yayy fun)...but just like any other shy teenage weeb, I never questioned why or how...I just went with the flow, like I always did.

> Mehh, who cares

was my mindset (since birth tbh >.<), but the deeper I explored the realm of possibilities around me, the more curious I got.

Mom used to say that I was always a curious kid since my childhood (like this one time i threw my report card in the dustbin..more on that in a future blog lol). But I never put in extra effort to strive for something, because it seemed like everything just fell into place by itself. Now that I've been shoved into a field I have zero prior knowledge about, I'm discovering things far beyond what my pea-sized brain can comprehend lol.

Everyday, I'm in awe of the spectacular things people do around me and I go around and look at them and I say to myself...

> Thats a sickkk idea, I wanna do it toooo

I've noticed that im finally having fun in whatever I'm doing...(Took me long enough hehe)

So here I am....A certified idiot who set out with a "Go with the flow" mindset, now finding himself standing in a sea of people who are some of the most smartest people he has ever met. Creative geniuses raised in this generation, where technical capabilities are the norm. My hot take, is that a big deal? Do you actually have to come out of the womb holding computers? Nah, I don't think so (ig).

---

Lemme break it down, I'd like to feel that I'm pretty ambitious (though my friends say otherwise, but anyways loll).

What I'm tryna say is that, I'm confident enough to say that I'm an equal competitor to everyone else....It's not just the determination or the grit that it takes to find a solution, you have to the right amount of crazy too, and in this day and age you can't argue that being juuusut a bit crazy is not good enough (are we all on board? Good :D)

Im a curious clown, always have been, but never in the right direction. Now that I've set myself a solid goal...I wanna learn, learn more and more and more things...Things I've never seen before.

As learning isnt limited, It all depends on how creative one can get to implement your thoughts into something real. Something that exists because you refused to leave it inside your head.

---

Theres a motto that i always follow...

> Stay curious long enough, and you'll eventually build something worth being proud of

Because, every time I achieve something, I'm outdoing my previous self, as I get better day by day. And thats something I can say that I'm proud of.

Looking back at the path that ive traced, It's not all bad that's happened...every decision, be it good or bad, has made me into who I am.

You dont notice how far you've gotten unless you look back and realize your progress, so thats what this is for.

## A reminder that I'll keep moving forward.
