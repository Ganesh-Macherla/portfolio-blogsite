Archeologists found symmetrical acheulean hand axes (don't ask me what that means), carefully shaped stone tools and controlled use of fire in several caves. Now, A symmetrical tool isn't reaaally necessary. A rough sharp rock would work just fine to make tools or to hunt. But these axes were often beautifully balanced and shaped. That suggests something other than survival, a mind that appreciated form, pattern, and refinement. Not just that, but they also found evidences that homo sapiens were experimenting and decorating new tools, exploring new environments, adapting quickly and sharing knowledge through stories, music and rituals (e.g. for burying the dead) via actual ancient languages or even hand signals. They found complex cave paintings with carved figurines and simple jewelry from shells, musical instruments made from bone. Cross verifying these traces with other sites across the world, they found instances of trade with other civilizations. Isn't that cool!!
