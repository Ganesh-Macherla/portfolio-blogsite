about.md

Greetings, explorer of the internet, and welcome to my domain — a place where I document projects, write random thoughts, overthink life decisions, and continuously convince myself that everything will work out if I stay motivated.

Meanwhile, deadlines pile up, my sleep schedule collapses, and my sanity slowly descends into chaos. But hey, it's fun. I promise :D

Most of what lives here started as a "let me just quickly try this" and turned into a repo. I like building things I can poke at — visualizers, gesture-controlled games, dashboards — and writing about whatever's rattling around in my head while I do it.
